## Compatibility and remaining design requests

- `App` is now a shared handle. `persistent_storage()` returns a cloned backend handle instead of a borrowed reference; a previously returned handle keeps its original backend after replacement.
- Windows WGPU defaults to DX12. Explicit Vulkan opt-in retains the reported platform-loader risk; this change does not claim to repair the Vulkan driver/loader. Native macOS/Linux behavior was not exercised on this Windows host.
- Canvas cache counters measure CPU preparation, including attempted batches that later fail to submit. Occupancy is shared per renderer and sampled at that canvas's last preparation. Bytes include geometry and estimated metadata, not just vertices times eight.
- Public retained mesh handles, retained scene replay and camera-uniform APIs remain separate design requests. This patch addresses the cache-thrashing defect and observability; it does not change geometry quality or promise a fixed hit ratio for every workload.
- Previously resolved reports (default mask glyph, modal/future redraw, close veto/native menus and the original absence of cross-frame Canvas meshes) remain historical reports. Their additional platform/design requests are not newly claimed as fixed here.
